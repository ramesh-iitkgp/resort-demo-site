Curated Adventures

Unforgettable Mandarmani Experiences

Mandarmani is famed for its 13-kilometer-long firm motorable beach—the longest in India. Here is what makes your getaway truly magical:

Drivable Beach Adventure

Feel the thrill of driving along the wide, firm sandy beach during low tide. Enjoy coastal photography with the roaring Bay waves beside you.

Beach Bonfire & Stargazing

Gather around a cozy beachside bonfire on chilly coastal evenings, serenaded by guitar melodies, acoustic waves, and sizzling tandoori skewers.

Water Sports & Parasailing

Get your adrenaline pumping with high-speed Jet Skis, Banana Boat rides, ATV quad biking, and coastal parasailing organized via our reception.

Red Crab Beach Walk

Witness Mandarmani's famous millions of red ghost crabs scurrying along the secluded beach stretches at dawn—a mesmerizing natural wonder.
